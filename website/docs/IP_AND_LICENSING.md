# Aether — IP and Licensing Record

Owner: Keystone Mere Ltd

## Aether Intellectual Property

Founder-created Aether intellectual property was assigned to
Keystone Mere Ltd under a written IP Assignment Agreement.

The company's IP Asset Register and executed assignment agreement
are the authoritative corporate records of that transfer.

## Future Contributions

Before incorporating third-party code, music, artwork, fonts or other
creative assets into a commercial Aether release:

1. Identify the creator or copyright owner.
2. Record the applicable licence or written assignment.
3. Confirm commercial use is permitted.
4. Record attribution or notice requirements.
5. Retain evidence of the licence or permission.

## Review

This record should be reviewed when:

- a significant dependency is introduced;
- Aether incorporates third-party media;
- an external contributor works on Aether;
- Aether approaches commercial release; or
- the distribution/licensing model changes.
