WHAT THIS VERSION DOES
- Reuses one scene component in editor and player.
- Encodes a bounded snapshot of visual settings in the URL fragment.
- Works without the original device's localStorage or an account.
- Uses existing hosted scene images; stays completely silent.
- Rejects invalid links and limits imported rendering settings.

LIMITS
- This is snapshot sharing, not server-backed publishing or access control.
- Changes after copying require a new link. Links cannot be revoked.
- Anyone holding a link can play and inspect its settings.
- No uploads, payment system, new scene collections, or audio playback yet.
- Scenes rely on the host's existing image assets and runtime remaining available.
